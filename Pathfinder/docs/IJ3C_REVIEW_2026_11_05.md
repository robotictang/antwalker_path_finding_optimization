# IJ3C manuscript review — 5 November 2026

## Assessment

The current manuscript meets the principal checked IJ3C presentation requirements. I recommend a focused scientific and editorial revision before submission. The main remaining issue is establishing the value of the study: it compares existing planning components and documents a simulator dependency, but does not demonstrate a reliable mean score advantage over the coordinate-based PSO control. Formatting compliance alone does not establish originality or acceptance.

The most useful revision would center the article on constrained body-route planning and the measured contribution/cost of its stages. Retain the motion experiment as a clearly bounded simulator diagnostic. Shorten repeated presentations of results and use the space for a clearer algorithm description, stronger comparison with relevant research and justification of the objective and evaluation design.

This is a review, not an edited manuscript. No scientific source, experiment, figure, manuscript or saved result was changed. No simulation or training was run.

## Findings that matter most

### 1. Make the research contribution explicit

The Introduction describes the system and repeats the main results, but gives limited explanation of the specific unresolved question in existing constrained route-planning work. Related Work relies mainly on foundational algorithms, two hexapod papers and benchmarking/statistics references. The list of nine references is not a detected template violation; its relevance and coverage are the issue.

Add a focused comparison with directly relevant constrained path-planning methods. Explain what each prior method optimizes, which constraints it enforces, what it assumes about initialization and what the present controlled comparison adds. Do not pad the reference list to an invented minimum or claim that no similar method exists without a literature search.

The paper's strongest present empirical pattern is the contribution of final refinement: V4 accepts 93/96 attempts against 90/96 without that stage, and its paired accepted scores are never higher in these recorded comparisons. However, incumbent retention is intended to accept improvements, so non-worsening is an expected design property. The measured size, consistency and computational cost are useful results; the direction alone is not a new optimization principle.

A more focused title to consider is **Constrained Route Planning for a Simulated Hexapod**. This is an editorial option, not a required journal title. Keep a clearly identified simulator diagnostic if body-assistance results remain in the article.

### 2. Keep the central statistical limitation prominent

Against PSO-C, V4's mean paired-map score difference is 0.690%, the median is zero and the descriptive 95% interval is [−0.255, 2.312]%. PSO-C accepts one more attempt. Omitting map 24 changes the remaining-map mean to −0.060%. These are material findings, not expendable caveats.

Keep the primary mean, interval, denominator, ties/losses and map-24 influence. The main result does not support a general claim that V4 is the superior planner. Positive intervals for some other comparisons are descriptive and unadjusted for five comparisons, as the article already states. Do not present those comparisons as corrected confirmatory significance findings.

### 3. Give the motion results a narrower role

The sixteen valid motion tests cover four previously evaluated maps under four conditions. They show completion in the full/half assistance conditions and timeouts in the zero-assistance conditions. The eight assisted executions report zero detected foot contacts under the specified six-foot detector. This is evidence of assistance dependence in this simulator, not validated unassisted walking or general controller robustness.

Retain the detector definition, its exclusion of other robot-part contacts, the four-map scope and the correction of the four incorrectly configured preliminary runs. The diagnostic can be substantially shorter without concealing any of these facts. Combine the completion/contact summaries and consider dropping the separate distance plot if the relevant distances remain summarized.

If the complete motion experiment were removed, all corresponding claims would also need removal from the title, abstract, methods, results, discussion and conclusions. Simply removing its unfavorable outcomes would be inappropriate. I recommend retaining a compact diagnostic instead.

### 4. Strengthen the method and objective explanation

Add a short pseudocode algorithm or compact flow diagram showing initial route construction, landmark search, refinement, constraint ranking and stopping rules. A small parameter table would make the existing defaults and alternative profiles easier to verify than the long prose lists. Explain noP/noL in the table legends or use descriptive labels such as “without final refinement” and “without landmark search.”

Explain the design rationale for the score denominators, battery coefficients and reserve rules. The nominal battery cost is constructed from distance, time and turning, so its contribution overlaps those route properties; it is not an independent measured energy outcome. This does not invalidate the defined score, but limits the physical interpretation. A controlled sensitivity study would strengthen robustness claims; it must be identified as new analysis/experimentation, not inserted as if already performed.

Explain why the hexapod application matters to the chosen footprint, speed and turn assumptions. The current planner is a two-dimensional body-route planner, not a planner of full three-dimensional foot placements. Preserve that distinction.

### 5. Keep computational comparisons appropriately scoped

All methods share a two-second nominal ceiling and a 240-route evaluation ceiling, but they do not perform equal actual work. PSO-C reaches the evaluation ceiling on 84/96 attempts while V4's median is 33 evaluated routes. Preparation is outside the method timers, and other desktop workloads were present. The existing text discloses these facts correctly.

Retain evaluation/clearance counts with timing and avoid a broad real-time speed claim. An additional budget sweep and an initial-route-only baseline would provide useful context if new experiments are undertaken. The [COCO experimental procedure](https://numbbo.github.io/coco-doc/experimental-setup/) treats initialization, tuning, termination and evaluation costs as part of algorithm benchmarking. It is supporting methodology, not an extra IJ3C submission rule.

## Revision priorities

1. Clarify the research gap and defensible contribution, and align the title/abstract/conclusions with it.
2. Add a compact algorithm description and clearer parameter/metric rationale.
3. Remove redundant displays while preserving the central comparisons and their limitations.
4. Condense the motion study into a bounded simulator diagnostic and retain its correction disclosure.
5. If further experimental work is feasible, prioritize a separately declared harder-map evaluation, a simple initial-route baseline and a controlled objective/budget sensitivity study. Preserve current results and label any new evidence accurately.
6. Complete author details and the already deferred supervisor decisions before submission; re-render and check the full edited document afterward.
