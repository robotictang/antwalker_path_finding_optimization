All 640 planning attempts finish without a logged  
trial error. Development selects the default profile for  
each  base  method.  Table  2  reports  main-test

6.2 Map types and computation
All  resource  rejections  occur  on  corridor  maps.  
Every method accepts 24 of 24 attempts in each of the 
open,  cluttered  and  rough  families.  Corridor  
acceptance counts are 21 of 24 for CLRP, PSO-P,  
ACO-P and CLRP noL; 22 of 24 for PSO-C; and 18 of 
24 for CLRP noP. Each family contains eight maps  
with three repetitions per map. These counts locate the 
additional  PSO-C  acceptance  and  the  three  
acceptances lost when final adjustment is removed.
Table 4 reports search effort across all 96 attempts  
per method. PSO-C reaches the 240-route evaluation  
limit in 84 of 96 attempts; no other method reaches this 
limit. Median route-evaluation counts are 33 for CLRP 
and 21 when landmark search is removed. Median  
geometric-clearance query counts are 1,033 for CLRP, 
2,142.5 for PSO-C and 146.5 for CLRP noL. Shared  
map  preparation  takes  a  median  of  0.715  s  and  a 
maximum of 0.895 s and is excluded from individual  
search timers. A route evaluation calculates metrics for 
an entire route; a clearance query can reject a segment 
before a complete route is evaluated.
