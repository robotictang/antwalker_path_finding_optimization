The  development  phase  contains  64  planning  
attempts: eight maps, four methods and two profiles.  
The main test contains 576 attempts: 32 maps, six  
methods and three paired repetitions. Each method  
therefore  has  96  main-test  attempts,  and  the  full  
experiment has 640 attempts. The maps, parameter-
selection criteria and primary analysis were defined  
before the main-test results. There was no external  
preregistration.  Acceptance  counts  include  all  
scheduled main-test attempts.
The three repetitions on each map use the same  
geometry  and  task  constraints  and  measure  
algorithmic variation within that map. They do not  
represent  three  independent  maps.  Paired  relative  
score differences are first averaged across mutually  
accepted  repetitions  within  each  map,  then  
summarized across maps. Each paired map therefore  
receives  equal  weight  even  when  the  number  of  
mutually accepted repetitions differs.

Following the resampling principle in [11], descriptive 
95%  percentile  bootstrap  intervals  use  5,000

Map-family  breakdowns,  computational  
summaries,  map-by-map  displays  and  omission  
analyses  were  calculated  after  the  main  test.  They 
describe  the  recorded  results  without  changing  the 
selected settings or main-test sample. In the omission  
analysis, one paired map is removed at a time and the  
mean is recalculated. The resulting means show each  
map’s influence on the average. All maps remain in  
the primary analysis.
Evaluations  run  on  Linux  with  Python  3.10.12,

5.4 Motion tests and contact measurements
The  motion  experiment  uses  four  previously  
assessed  maps:  one  each  from  the  open,  cluttered, 
rough  and  corridor  families,  with  seeds  1670000–
1670003. Each mission follows the CLRP route from  
the map’s first repetition and then the fixed return  
route to a charging station. Outward route lengths are  
21.36, 22.30, 23.45 and 19.78 m, respectively. Initial  
nominal battery levels are 45%, 85%, 45% and 100%, 
respectively.  These  first-repetition  routes  form  a  
separate diagnostic from the 32 main-test maps.
Each mission is executed under four conditions: full 
body assistance with gravity compensation, half-scale 
assistance  with  compensation,  zero  assistance  with 
compensation,  and  zero  assistance  without  
compensation.  All  conditions  use  the  same  path  
tracker and a maximum simulated execution time of  
180 s. Completion requires reaching the destination  
and returning to the station. Unfinished missions time 
out at 180 s; this execution limit is distinct from the  
planner’s outward-route deadline. Completed mission 
times  include  eight  seconds  of  simulated  station  
service.
Gravity compensation is verified after initialization 
of the locomotion  controller. The no-compensation  
condition sets all leg-body compensation values to  
zero at that point. Four initial executions in which  
initialization  restored  compensation  were  excluded  
and rerun after its removal was verified. The final  
comparison  contains  16  executions:  four  missions  
under  each  of  four  conditions.  These  previously  
assessed routes provide a diagnostic of the execution  
model rather than a new test set for locomotion.
Foot contact is logged at every 0.004 s physics step. 
Each  of  the  six  foot  geometries  is  counted  as  
contacting an external geometry when the reported  
contact distance is at most 0.002 m. Mean contact feet 
is the average number of feet detected in contact per  
sample.  The  no-contact  fraction  is  the  fraction  of  
samples with no detected foot contacts. The detector  
covers only the six feet; contacts involving other robot 
components are excluded from these counts.
