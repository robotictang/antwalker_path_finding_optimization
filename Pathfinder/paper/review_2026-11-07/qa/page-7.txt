Once the locomotion controller is initialised, we  
check the gravity compensation. At that point, the no-
compensation  condition  sets  all  leg-body  
compensation values to zero. The first four executions, 
where  initialisation  restored  compensation,  were  
omitted and rerun after confirming that compensation  
was  removed.  Finally,  the  comparison  includes  16 
runs, four missions for each of the four conditions. The 
pathways evaluated previously provide an analysis of  
the execution framework, not a new testing array for  
movement.
Each physics interval of 0.004 second captures foot 
contact. If the measured contact distance is less than  
0.002  m,  then  any  of  the  six  foot  geometries  are  
deemed to be in contact with an external geometry.  
Mean contact feet is the average number of feet found 
in contact for each sample. The no-contact fraction is  
the percent of samples where no foot contacts are  
found. The detector only picks up the six feet. Counts  
do not include interactions with  other parts  of the  
robot.
The body tilt is calculated from the body alignment. 
Assisted executions modify directly the roll and pitch  
angular  velocities  by  using  a  feedback  of  
orientation.The  assisted  controller  behaviour  is  
characterised by a low tilt. Positive actuator work is  
computed by multiplying actuator force times actuator 
velocity . This only takes positive power, which is  
integrated over time and summed over all actuators . It 
does not include work due to instantaneous changes in 
the velocity of the body. These metrics characterise the 
model  motion  and  actuator  performance  under  the 
specified assistance parameters.
