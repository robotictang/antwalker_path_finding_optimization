8 Conclusions
The  final  local  refinement  improves  acceptance  
from 90 to 93 out of 96 trials on 32 primary test maps. 
Landmark search is still employed, and it provides a  
conditional  mean  paired-map  relative  score  
improvement of 0.924% over the case of not using it,  
while the average search time increases from 0.196  
seconds to 0.376 seconds. Comparing the two stage  
removal methods shows different trade-offs between  
acceptance, score and computation. The research does 
not demonstrate a superiority of mean score against  
PSO-C. The mean paired-map relative enhancement is 
0.690%, the median is zero and the descriptive 95%  
bootstrap interval contains zero. PSO-C allows one  
additional attempt.
There are 16 valid motion executions which enable  
independent diagnosis. All four missions are achieved 
successfully  with  both  full-scale  and  half-scale  
support.  Both  zero-assistance  settings  return  no  
completions and timeout at 180 seconds, regardless of 
whether  verified  leg-body  gravity  compensation  is  
present. The established six-foot criterion shows no  
foot contact for all assisted executions. This research  
therefore  separates  the  acceptance  of  a  restricted  
bodily  route  from  the  approval  of  unassisted  
movement. The mentioned costs and trade-offs are  
appropriate for the given simulation and objective.
