6.3 Variation across maps
Figure 2 shows the relative score improvements of  
all 31 paired maps over PSO-C, which spans from  
−1.718%  to  23.202%.  Map  24  shows  the  largest  
positive effect. Removing it changes the mean of the  
remaining 30 paired maps from the full sample mean  
of  0.690%  to  −0.060%.  The  leave-one-map-out  
approach for noP comparison presents a positive result 
between  0.682%  and  0.822%.  These  retrospective  
summaries assess impact on the unmodified primary  
sample.

Map 24 continues to be included in the main dataset 
used to calculate the mean and bootstrap interval. Its  
effect accounts for the positive mean, zero median and 
equal number of pairs with lower and higher scores,  
compared  with  PSO-C.  The  exclusion  calculation  
measures the concentration of the outcome. This is not 
a reason to exclude the map from the main analysis.
6.4 Selected route comparison
Map 24 is in the corridor family. Both techniques  
handle all three iterations. CLRP is able to generate the 
same route with a distance of 20.803 m and a score of 
1.310 in every iteration. The average distance for PSO-
C is 27.135m and the average score is 1.706. For PSO-
C, the expected time, normal battery cost and exposure 
are  32.959  seconds,  15.446  percentage  points  and  
1.012 for CLRP, respectively.
All four score components are lower in CLRP for  
each  of  the  paired  repetitions.  Figure  3  shows  
repetition zero. PSO-C has a longer trajectory toward  
the west. This helps to explain the variability in the  
scores of the routes returned, but does not identify any 
particular internal component of the optimizer.
