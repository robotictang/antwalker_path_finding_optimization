Guo et al. [7] propose a hybrid approach for mobile 
robots trajectory planning based on particle swarm  
optimization  and  control  barrier  function  velocity  
adjustments. Their safety mechanism ties planning to  
speed  control.  The  current  comparison  retains  the  
segment-clearance test, and compares acceptance and  
score under the same resource constraints. It does not  
run, or evaluate, their controller. This is important  
because changing the collision model, initialization or 
execution method will affect both the comparison and 
the optimizer.
Also  the  hexapod  investigations  lead  to  better  
measurements below the body-route level. Yin et al.  
improve  swing  trajectories,  gait  timing  and  gait  
sequence on  LittleStrong  [8].  Chen  et al.  integrate  
topographical  characteristics  with  travel  expenses,  
foot  positioning  and  movement  strategies  in  both  
simulated and experimental studies [9]. The current  
work  presents  a  static  gait  controller,  the  input  of

3 Robot and planning model
3.1 Robot model and body assistance
The robot model has six limbs, each of which has  
three movable joints. The imported model has a total  
mass of 97.52 kg and a base mass of 35 kg. In the built 
MuJoCo model, the total robot mass is 113.32kg and  
the  base  mass  is  46kg.  The  assembly  consists  of  
wheels  and  a  scanner  .  23  actuators  in  total  .  All 
experiments are in walking mode; wheels and scanner 
are included in the simulated mass.
The MuJoCo robot is shown in a static picture in its 
ambulation  pose  in  Figure  1.  From  the  stationary  
perspective the assembly with the wheels and scanner 
is visible. The adjacent obstacles are hidden for clarity. 
The results of the planning in Section 6 make use of  
the maps and task restrictions described in Section 5.

The imported robot has specifications of 60 Nm  
torque and 20 rad/s angular velocity for the leg joints  
constraints.  These  are  defined  limits  in  the  
specification. The study does not include any actual  
measurements of motor torque-speed characteristics,  
battery depletion or robot weight. Here the platform  
under consideration is the constructed simulation, with 
the imported mass values differentiating the source  
model from the assembled model.
