complete all four missions. None of the zero-assistance

The  six-foot  contact  evaluation  shows  all  eight  
assisted  executions  with  no  foot  contact  at  any  
measured  physics  interval.  The  results  suggest  
navigation with direct bodily support but no evidence  
for propulsion by foot-ground interaction. The model  
can move a short distance on its own, but it does not  
accomplish  any  of  the  four  tasks  in  180  seconds,  
whether gravity compensation is on or confirmed off.  
The  results  describe  the  four  missions  that  were  
evaluated, but do not provide an assessment of an  
overall hardware failure rate.
7 Discussion
The final local modification improves acceptance  
and leads to better scores on more paired accepted  
trials  than  landmark  search  given  the  parameters  
tested.  Taking  it  out  loses  three  acceptances  on  
corridor  maps,  whereas  skipping  landmark  search  
keeps  the  acceptance  but  reduces  the  computation  
recorded.  The  retention  of  incumbents  allows  for  
improvements  in  ranking  only,  so  a retained  route  
cannot get worse in the final phase. The empirical  
result measures the trade-offs among the parameters  
selected. Independent stage removal scheduling does  
not  guarantee  uniform  search  histories  across  
iterations.  A  baseline  using  the  initial  route  and  a 
budget-matched comparison would help to separate  
the quality of initialisation from the impact of later  
phases; such comparisons are not made here.
The research does not show superiority of mean  
scores from CLRP over PSO-C. The average gain in  
the paired-map relative score is 0.690% but the median 
is still zero and the 95% bootstrap confidence interval 
contains zero. If a range contains zero, that does not  
imply equality or subordination. PSO-C permits one  
more trial and the map 24 has great impact on the  
favourable mean relative improvement. The PSO-P  
versus  ACO-P  comparisons  show  positive  mean  
relative  improvements  and  no  median  paired-map  
effects, which are the results of the configurations  
tested.  Differences  in  initialisation  and  route  
representation  prevent  attributing  all  differences  to 
representation  alone.  The  five  intervals  are  not  
corrected  for  multiple  comparisons  and  thus  are  
descriptive rather than confirmatory.
