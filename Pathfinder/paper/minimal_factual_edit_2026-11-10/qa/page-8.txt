6.3 Variation across maps
All 31 relative score improvements achieved with  
paired-maps  are  shown  in  figure  2,  from  minus  
1.718% to plus 23.202%. The highest gain is on Map  
24. The full 31-map mean is 0.690%; omitting Map 24 
gives −0.060% over the remaining 30 maps.  For the  
noP  comparison,  it  is  leave-one-map-out,  remain  
positive, ranging from 0.682% to 0.822%. These post  
hoc summaries are computed as influence within the  
unchanged primary sample.
Figure 2. Average over jointly accepted repetitions of

Map 24 is still in the main dataset that the mean and 
bootstrap interval is computed from. This map drives 
the positive mean, which coexists with a zero map-
level  median  and  equal  attempt-level  lower-  and  
higher-score  counts  against  PSO-C.  Omission  
calculation is a measure of concentration of the result. 
It is not sufficient to warrant the exclusion of the map 
from the main analysis.
6.4 Selected route comparison
All  three  repetitions  are  acceptable  for  both  
methods. In the CLRP, the route is repeated the same  
distance in each repetition, 20.803 m, and score 1.310. 
PSO-C has a mean distance of 27.135 m and a mean  
score  of  1.706.  CLRP's  predicted  time,  nominal  
battery  cost  and  exposure  are  32.959  s,  15.446  
percentage points and 1.012, respectively, compared  
to PSO-C means of 42.584 s, 20.066 percentage points 
and  1.665.  CLRP  scores  are  lower  in  all  four  
components for each paired repetition. Repetition zero 
is shown in Figure 3. PSO-C goes down a longer road 
to the west. This is why you are seeing the scores of  
the returned routes vary, but it does not specify one  
particular internal factor in the optimizer.
