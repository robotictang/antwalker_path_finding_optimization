# Review of reader dependencies and factual wording

The previous article contained repeated references to saved or archived inputs, a manifest, source/model hashes, supplementary records and internal arithmetic audits. It also described data/code availability before any public deposit existed. These passages were removed from the separate manuscript. The study design, counts, selection rules and replay settings are now explained in the article rather than delegated to those files.

The rewrite uses accepted route for a plan that meets the geometric and resource tests, and body assistance for direct velocity corrections. It explains the return battery allowance, score ranking, numerical tie tolerance, timing scope and foot-contact detector. Imported joint limits are explicitly attributed to the imported robot description, rather than presented as physical measurements or verified simulator torque-speed behavior.

The original planning outcomes remain unchanged. A new illustration uses map 24, the map with the largest positive paired effect against PSO-C. Its five route metrics are calculated directly for both methods over all three repetitions, and the figure uses repetition zero. The text states the post-hoc selection rule and reports the full effect distribution beside the example. No unique internal cause of the optimizer's route choice is asserted.

The paper retains bootstrap intervals because they are calculated study results. It also states that costs are nominal, motion is assisted and physical validation is absent. Removing these facts would imply support that the experiment does not provide. Vague predictions and speculative explanations are omitted.

There is no acknowledgment or public data-availability promise in the manuscript. Disclosure placement, author/contact information and an actual data deposit remain matters for the authors; these administrative details are kept outside the research text.

The six-foot contact detector, assistance correction and all primary source/data hashes were checked against the preceding evidence. Checks also reconcile the new map-specific table, figure/table numbering and absence of local-file wording. The related-work statements about Smart Gait, terrain planning and COCO were checked against the publishers' articles and the official COCO procedure:

- https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s10033-024-01000-0
- https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neurorobotics/articles/10.3389/fnbot.2025.1605938/full
- https://numbbo.github.io/coco-doc/experimental-setup/

The previous ten-page manuscript and the frozen planning campaign are preserved unchanged. This editorial review does not certify journal acceptance or scientific novelty.
