# Publication readiness after the revision

This is a substantially stronger **simulation-only review draft**, not a certified submission-ready paper. The target is the International Journal of Computer, Consumer and Control (IJ3C). Its [official author guide](https://ij3c.ncut.tw/#authors) lists English, generally at most 10,000 words, A4, two columns, Times New Roman at least 10 pt, a PDF including figures/tables, and source files after acceptance. The downloaded instructions additionally specify a <=250-word abstract, no more than five keywords and numbered references. The legacy sample is retained for transparency; no current submission portal or fee was inferred from unrelated download links.

## What is ready for human review

The revised article is focused on representation effects, component trade-offs and the boundary between assisted simulation and physical validity. It includes a new locally frozen campaign, a stronger coordinate control, an explicit adverse result, source/geometry checks, a mass discrepancy audit and an assistance-removal diagnostic. Original evidence was preserved. The paper's numerical claims are tied to archived records and its limitations are explicit.

The continuous-coordinate control admits one more primary attempt than V4, and its paired interval spans zero. This should remain visible in the abstract, discussion and conclusions. Do not change the title or interpretation to promise superiority. A negative or modest result can still inform an engineering comparison, but the authors must explain why that knowledge is useful and distinct from existing work.

## Remaining author decisions

1. Confirm the contribution and journal fit with the supervisor, particularly whether a comparative simulation case study is sufficiently useful for the intended readership.
2. Supply and agree author names/order, affiliations, corresponding email, funding and conflicts. These remain pending at the user's request.
3. Verify the closest related literature and each reference against the original source. Several publisher pages blocked automated access; available metadata and earlier citations do not substitute for reading the relevant papers. This revision does not claim a comprehensive new literature search.
4. Independently inspect the code and reproduce the new analysis in a clean scientific environment. Existing audits are internal checks, not independent peer replication.
5. Decide whether more independent map sources or a workload-controlled timing campaign is needed for the contribution. More repetitions from the same generator alone do not establish broad generalization.
6. Assign a persistent public evidence location and check the current journal submission procedure before submitting. No public upload, copyright acceptance, payment or journal submission was performed.

## Physical claims remain unavailable

There is no hardware access or external electrical data. The 97.52 kg source/113.32 kg simulation discrepancy is documented, not corrected by guessing. Replacing those values without measurements would create another assumed model. Zero chassis assistance timed out on all four diagnostic routes; that does not establish a real robot's behavior, but it directly prevents treating the assisted demonstration as proof of autonomous locomotion.

Keep claims about **modeled route score, admission and explicitly assisted simulation**. Claims about real battery savings, actuator capability or stable autonomous walking require measurements and a different validation stage. A publisher change does not supply that missing evidence.
