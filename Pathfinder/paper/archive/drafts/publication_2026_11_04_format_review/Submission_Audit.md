## Scientific and editorial checks

The record/geometry audit passes all 640 planning records, 187 distinct routes and 748 recomputed metric values. A separate claim check reconciles admissions, recorded mean times, paired outcomes, model specifications, component results and the 16 diagnostic executions. No frozen scientific source or primary record was changed during this formatting review. The [evidence map](Evidence_Map.md) identifies the supporting artifacts for the retained claims.

The article removes review-status notices, administrative instructions, generalized advice and the demonstration screenshot. It uses a frozen first-case route figure, native mathematical fractions/subscripts and direct methods/results prose. It reports that the coordinate control changes initialization as well as representation, so the comparison does not isolate a representation-only causal effect.

Claims of universal superiority, global optimality, measured electrical savings, physical walking stability and hardware failure probability are excluded. Statistical intervals and model limitations remain because they define the supported inference. All descriptive score effects remain conditional on joint admission and the declared map distribution.
