7 Discussion
Final  local  adjustment  improves  acceptance  and  
yields lower scores in more paired accepted attempts  
than  landmark  search  under  the  tested  settings.  
Removing it loses three acceptances on corridor maps; 
removing landmark search preserves acceptance and  
reduces  recorded  computation.  Incumbent  retention 
permits only improvements in rank, so a retained route 
cannot worsen during the final stage. The empirical  
result  quantifies  the  trade-offs  under  the  selected  
settings. Separately timed stage-removal trials do not  
guarantee identical search histories across repetitions.  
An initial-route-only baseline and a matched-budget  
comparison would help separate initialization quality  
from  the  contribution  of  later  stages;  those  
comparisons are not included here.
The  study  does  not  demonstrate  a  mean  score  
advantage for CLRP over PSO-C. The mean paired-
map relative score improvement is 0.690%, but its  
descriptive 95% bootstrap interval includes zero and  
the  median  is  zero.  An  interval  spanning  zero  
establishes  neither  equality  nor  inferiority.  PSO-C  
accepts one additional attempt, and map 24 strongly  
influences the positive mean relative improvement.  
The CLRP-versus-PSO-P and  CLRP-versus-ACO-P  
comparisons  have  positive  mean  relative  
improvements and zero median paired-map effects;  
these  results  apply  to  the  tested  configurations.  
Differences in initialization and route representation  
prevent attribution to representation alone. The five  
intervals are unadjusted for multiple comparisons and 
are descriptive rather than confirmatory evidence.
All  methods  accept  every  tested  non-corridor  
attempt,  so  acceptance  differences  are  confined  to  
corridor  instances  within  the  four  specified  map  
families. The three repetitions measure algorithmic  
variation within maps and do not increase the number 
of  independent  main-test  maps  beyond  32.  More  
complex or unknown environments require a separate 
evaluation. Sensitivity to score weights and reserve  
settings is not evaluated. The combined score contains 
overlapping route preferences because nominal battery 
use also depends on distance and duration. Actuator  
work  excludes  work  introduced  by  direct  body  
assistance, and no electrical measurements validate  
physical battery savings. Search times describe the  
recorded shared-desktop setting and exclude shared  
preparation;  they  are  not  deployment  latency  
measurements.
The motion diagnostic establishes dependence on  
direct body assistance for the four tested missions.  
Assistance  corrects  body  velocities  and  roll/pitch  
rates,  so  completed  missions  and  low  tilt  do  not  
independently validate a walking gait. The six-foot  
contact measurements further limit that interpretation. 
Known  map  and  pose  inputs,  different  source  and 
assembled masses, and the absence of physical motor, 
battery, terrain and contact measurements define the  
simulation’s scope. Physical walking and sensor-based 
navigation are not evaluated.
8 Conclusions
On  32  main-test  maps,  final  local  refinement  
increases acceptance from 90 to 93 of 96 attempts.  
Landmark search preserves acceptance and gives a  
conditional  mean  paired-map  relative  score  
improvement of 0.924% compared with its removal,  
while increasing mean search time from 0.196 to 0.376 
s. The two stage-removal comparisons therefore show 
different  trade-offs  between  acceptance,  score  and  
computation. The study does not demonstrate a mean  
score advantage over PSO-C: the mean paired-map  
relative improvement is 0.690%, the median is zero,  
and the descriptive 95% bootstrap interval includes  
zero. PSO-C accepts one additional attempt.
