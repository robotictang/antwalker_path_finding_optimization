6.2 Map types and computation
All corridor Map resource rejections are made on  
the corridor Map. All methods make 24 of 24 attempts 
in each of the open, cluttered and rough families. The  
corridor acceptance rate is 21 of 24 for CLRP, 22 of  
24 for PSO-P, 18 of 24 for CLRP noP and 24 of 24 for 
ACO-P. There are eight maps per family each repeated 
three times. These counts identify the extra PSO-C  
acceptances  and  the three  acceptances that are not  
there  when  final  adjustment  is  eliminated.  Search  
effort  for  all  96  search  efforts for  each  method  is  
reported  in  Table  4.  In  84/96  attempts,  PSO-C

search  timers.  While  a  route  evaluation  provides
