The development phase includes 64 planning attempts 
(8 maps, 4 methods and 2 profiles). The main test has  
576 shots: 32 maps, 6 methods and 3 repetitions for  
each method. Thus there are 96 main-test attempts  
each method and 640 for the whole experiment. Before 
the results of the main tests, maps, parameter-selection 
criteria, and primary analysis were set. No external  
preregistration was done. All scheduled attempts were 
included in the acceptance count, including all main-
test attempts. The three repetitions of each map have  
the same geometry and task constraints and quantify  
algorithmic variation within each map. These are NOT 
three  separate  maps.  These  differences  in  relative  
score are averaged across all repetitions where they are 
mutually accepted in each map and then summarized  
across all maps. Thus the number of mutual accepted  
repetitions is not considered as weight when using a  
paired map.
5.3 Score comparison and statistical analysis
Only attempts in which a route is accepted by both  
the CLRP and comparison method are compared. Only 
one  method  of  acceptance  and  both  methods  of  
rejection count. The relative score difference for a  
paired attempt is defined by equation (5). Qc is the  
score for the comparison method and Qv is the score

The reported mean and median are the averages of  
the  paired  relative  score  differences  on  the  map.  
Descriptive  95%  percentile  bootstrap  intervals  are  
based on 5,000 resamples of maps with random seed  
41104, following the resampling principle in [11]. The 
repetitions are still in the same map. The intervals are  
subject to acceptance by both methods and are not  
adjusted  for  the  five  comparisons.  A  relative-
improvement threshold is used only for descriptive  
purposes  of  counting  larger  improvements:  0.5%.  
Map-family  breakdowns,  computational  summaries  
and map-by-map displays and omission analyses were 
performed following the main test. They record results 
without altering the set-up or main-test sample. One  
paired map is omitted at a time in the omission analysis 
and the mean is calculated. The resulting means is the 
average influence of each map. All maps are used in  
the  main  analysis.  Evaluations  run  on  Linux  with  
Python  3.10.12,  MuJoCo  3.9.0,  NumPy  2.2.6  and  
SciPy 1.15.3. The processor is an Intel Core i5-7600,  
3.50 GHz and 4 logical processors. Many processes  
are executed at the same time on the desktop. This  
shared-desktop configuration is reported in recorded  
wall times, and is not exclusive processor usage or  
deployment latency.
5.4 Motion tests and contact measurements
The four maps used in the motion experiment have  
seeds 1670000 to 1670003. Each mission takes the  
CLRP path on the first repetition of the map and the  
set path back to a charging station. Outward route  
lengths  are  21.36,  22.30,  23.45  and  19.78  m,  
respectively.  The  initial  nominal  battery  levels  are 
45%, 85%, 45% and 100% respectively. The first-
repetition routes are a stand-alone diagnostic to the 32 
main-test maps. This is done in four conditions: full  
body  with  gravity  compensation,  half  scale  with  
gravity compensation, zero with gravity compensation 
and zero without gravity compensation. The same path 
tracker  is  used  for  all  conditions  and  a  maximum 
simulated  execution  time  of  180  s.  Completion  is  
defined as getting to the destination and back to the  
station. Unfinished missions time out at 180 s, a time  
limit that is different from the planner's outward-route 
time limit. Mission times are completed and includes  
eight seconds of simulated station  service.  Gravity  
compensation  is  checked  once  the  locomotion  
controller has been initialized. The no-compensation  
condition makes all the leg-body compensation values 
zero at that point. Four initial executions where the  
compensation was restored by the initialization were  
not included and rerun once the removal was verified. 
The final comparison consists of 16 missions, all four  
missions  under  each  of  four  conditions.  These  are 
routes that have been evaluated and are not a new test 
set  for  locomotion,  but  rather  a  diagnostic  of  the  
execution model. Foot contact is recorded for each  
Physics  step  (0.004  s).  Contacts  with  external  
geometries are reported if the contact distance is 0.002 
m or less, with each of the six foot geometries being  
considered as contacting an external geometry. Mean  
contact feet = average number of feet found in contact 
per  sample.  No  contact  fraction  is  the  fraction  of  
samples in which there are no foot contacts found.  
Only the six feet of the detector count, contacts with  
other parts of the robot do not count. Body tilt is  
derived from the body orientation. Low tilt refers to  
the behavior of the assisted controller and is a direct  
adjustment of roll and pitch angular velocities based  
on the orientation feedback. Positive actuator work is  
determined by multiplying the actuator force by the  
actuator  velocity,  keeping  only  positive  power,  
integrating over time and summing over actuators. It
