OLD P005 In this work, we evaluate landmark search and final local modification in body-route planning for a simulated hexapod. Six configurations share obstacle-clearance, deadline, nominal battery and terrain-exposure constraints. Parameter selection consists of 64 trials over eight development maps; the main test consists of 576 trials over 32 maps, giving 640 planning trials. The complete planner, Constrained Landmark Route Planning (CLRP), accepts 93 of 96 main-test attempts, compared with 94 for coordinate-based particle swarm optimization, PSO-C. Removing final adjustment reduces acceptance to 90. Without landmark search, 93 acceptances are retained and mean search time falls from 0.376 to 0.196 s. Complete CLRP has a conditional mean paired-map relative score improvement of 0.924% over this reduced version. Against PSO-C, the mean paired-map relative score improvement is 0.690%, with a zero median and a descriptive 95% bootstrap interval of [−0.255, 2.312]%. This interval includes zero and does not demonstrate a mean score advantage. A separate diagnostic consists of 16 executions: four missions under four assistance conditions. Full and half-scale assistance each complete all four missions. Both zero-assistance conditions complete none and time out at 180 s. Assisted executions record no foot contact under the six-foot contact test. The results quantify planning trade-offs and dependence on direct body assistance. Nominal battery costs are model outputs without physical or electrical validation.
